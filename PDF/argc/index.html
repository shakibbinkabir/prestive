Arunima Resort Golf Club Membership details.

Types of Membership:

1.    Permanent Member- Permanent Members will have to pay subscription fees annually or monthly whichever method is convenient to them.

2.    Life Member- Life members will have to pay one time registration fee and no subscription fees to ARGC.

3.    Corporate Member- Corporate Membership is provided to top executives of organizations and they can provide the membership to maximum five of their personnel in ex-officio basis at one time. Individual members have to pay their subscription fees annually or monthly whichever method is convenient to them. However, for every change of personnel the organization has to pay additional fees.

4.    Honorary Membership- Honorary Membership is provided to Government High Officials and prominent personalities. Honorary membership is complimentary and need not to pay monthly or annual fees. Honorary members will enjoy all facilities of Life and General Members but cannot be a member of the Executive Committee.

5.    General Member – General members and their family can only use ARGC facilities and other affiliated clubs except clubs affiliated with BGF. Maximum 2 children of Life/ Permanent members can avail General Membership at 30% discount on published rate.

6.    Foreign Membership- Foreign Membership is provided to people who are citizens of other countries. They cannot be

members of the Executive Committee. 25% of the valid market rate to be paid as renewal fee after every 3 years.

Requirements for Membership:

1.    06 Copies Passport size lab print photographs

2.    Membership Application form

3.    Bio-Data with photograph

4.    Photocopy of National ID

5.    Photocopy of all passports (If dual citizenship)

6.    TIN      Certificate      and     Photocopy     of      last      three             years Acknowledgement Receipt (Not applicable for foreign members)

7.    Updated Trade License (In case of Corporate Member)

8.    Photocopy of Work permit and Visa (Applicable for Foreigners)

However, the authority reserves the right to change the above privileges at any time.

ii.            As ARGC is an affiliated member of Bangladesh Golf Federation (BGF), members of ARGC shall abide BGF laws while visiting other clubs affiliated with BGF. Membership application may be scrutinized by DGFI.

iii.            Our club members will be able to use facilities of local and foreign clubs that we are affiliated with or will be affiliated with and has to follow their club rules while visiting those clubs. However, any misconduct occurring by any member at any affiliated club may render his/her membership terminated.

iv.            The ARGC Golf Club Committee will be selected by the board of Directors of Shabaz Tourism Ltd. Shabaz Tourism Ltd. is the mother Company of Arunima Resort Golf Club.

v.            The Membership is transferable subjected to a Commission of 20% on the valid market rate, payable to the club during the transfer date. The new membership approval will be subjected to scrutiny.

vi.            The club management reserves the right to change its rates and tariffs at any time.

vii.            The facilities of Membership would be available to the member and his/her family members only. The children of Members are entitled to the privileges until the age of 21. However, they may avail individual membership at 50% discount from market price.

viii.            The Member is not owner of Arunima Resort Golf Club (ARGC) or shareholder of Shabaz Tourism Ltd. Hence, they can never claim ownership to any of the mentioned business organizations.

ix.            Subscription fee is to be paid by

a.      Permanent Member either in monthly instalments of BDT 600 or an annual fee of BDT 4800.

b.     Corporate member either in monthly instalments of BDT 400 or an annual fee of BDT 3500.

c.      Honorary members will avail all facilities of General and life Members except they cannot be members of Club EC committee. Honorary Members do not have to pay Registration or Subscription fees.

d.     All members except Honorary Members & Life Members, shall pay all BGF fees in addition to ARGC fees after getting BGF ID.

e.      Foreign Members has to pay a membership renewal fee after every three years. The renewal fees will be 25% of the published foreign membership purchase rate.

x.            Foreign members must submit their passport (Photocopy)

xi.            If any member is convicted by the highest court of the country, his membership will automatically be terminated. Also, if any member violates rules and regulations of ARGC, disciplinary action will be taken against him/her.
